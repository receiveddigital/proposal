The Situation

University of Lynchburg processes approximately 77,000 mail items per year — roughly 50,000 packages through Qtrak and 27,000 letters with zero tracking, zero chain of custody, and zero student notification. Qtrak's two-step receive-then-route workflow requires approximately 36 seconds per package (18 seconds per step × 2 steps) and leaves the letter mail operation completely blind.

The result: staff investigation time for problem packages, a "deliver all" button that generates routing errors, dual-barcode SurePost confusion creating phantom records, and no defensible answer when a student or parent asks where their letter is.

Qtrak requires two separate steps to process every package — a receive step and a route step. Each involves scanning a barcode, manually typing the first several letters of a name, and selecting the correct recipient from a list. At approximately 18 seconds per step, the full two-step workflow averages 36 seconds per package.

Beyond the Numbers — Non-Quantified Value

"Deliver All" Error Eliminated
Destin specifically cited Qtrak's "deliver all" button as a source of operational errors — students accidentally triggering mass confirmation or staff inadvertently marking all items delivered. This error class is eliminated entirely.
SurePost Phantom Duplicates Gone
SurePost packages carry both a FedEx and USPS barcode. Scanning both in Qtrak creates duplicate phantom records. Received Digital reads and associates all barcodes to a single item record — no duplicates, no system noise.
Campus Delivery Integration
University of Lynchburg's active campus delivery subscription program (Square POS) can embed the Square delivery link directly in Received Digital item notifications — reducing manual follow-up steps for staff.
Locker Transition Ready
Destin noted interest in transitioning to a locker system. Received Digital's multi-barcode capture resolves the common locker integration failure point — premature barcode scan capturing the wrong code — making that eventual transition cleaner.
~70% Pickup Rate Improvement
Image-rich notifications — students see a photo of their actual item — have driven pickup rate increases of approximately 70% at comparable institutions. Less congestion, less aged inventory, fewer overflowing mailboxes requiring manual outreach.
Institutional Risk Eliminated
Donor checks, financial aid documents, driver's licenses, SSN cards, health services correspondence — currently zero chain of custody. Full imaging + IMb barcode reading + AI assignment means the "we can't prove we received it" problem is eliminated permanently.
