Executive Summary

The Problem in Plain Terms

GCU's letter mail workflow requires three manual steps for every single piece — a physical date/time stamp, a dual database lookup across Qtrak and an Excel spreadsheet, and a manual three-bucket sort. At 500 letters per day, that is approximately 130,000 pieces per year processed through a workflow that has no learning, no chain of custody, and no way to answer "where is my letter?" with any certainty.

The result: excessive staff time on repetitive decisioning, no defensible audit trail for high-stakes mail (checks, tax/financial documents, immigration paperwork), and a system that gets harder to manage — not easier — as enrollment grows.

What Changes with Received Digital

Received Digital collapses all three current steps into a single action at the workstation. The moment a letter is imaged, it is timestamped, the recipient is auto-assigned using AI-powered name matching, and it is routed to the correct category — with no additional keystrokes. Name variations and exceptions are resolved once; the system learns permanently and never asks the same question twice.

Phase 2 Opportunity

Package Unification — The Natural Next Step

This analysis is scoped to letter mail only, which is GCU's primary and most urgent pain point. However, the bigger picture is worth framing clearly.

GCU is the single inbound delivery point for a campus of 15,000–19,000 resident students and approximately 30,000 faculty and staff — processing an estimated 130,000 letters and 500,000 packages per year through a single mail center. Today, those two streams run on separate systems with separate workflows, separate training, and separate vendor relationships.

Full Platform Unification

One system. Every piece. Every recipient.

A fully unified Received Digital platform covering both letter mail and packages at GCU's confirmed volumes is estimated at $69,870 annually — a $29,870 increase over the current Qtrak spend, but delivering something Qtrak never has and never will: a single image-based system for every inbound piece, every audit trail, and real-time reporting on demand.
