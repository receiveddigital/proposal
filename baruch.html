Executive Summary

The Opportunity

Baruch College processes 135,000 letter mail pieces annually for faculty and staff across six campus buildings — with zero tracking capability. Every piece is sorted manually, routed from staff memory, and delivered with no proof of completion. When faculty ask where their mail is, the mailroom cannot answer. When delivery is disputed, there is no record to reference.

Faculty and staff mail tends to be higher-value correspondence, including checks, legal documents, grant-related materials, and vendor contracts. Received Digital solves this with AI-powered image capture, automated recipient assignment, image-based notifications, and a faculty self-service portal — transforming a fully manual, accountability-free process into a modern, verifiable workflow.
